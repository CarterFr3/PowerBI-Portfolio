# Supply Chain Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
This Power BI project analyzes a fashion & beauty supply chain dataset. The goal is to identify cost drivers, supplier performance issues, lead time problems, and opportunities to improve efficiency and profitability.

## Business Questions Answered
- Which product types generate the most revenue and gross profit?
- How do lead times vary across suppliers?
- Which transportation modes drive the highest shipping costs?
- Which SKUs are the most profitable?
- Are we meeting our target lead time?

## Skills Demonstrated
- Data modeling (Star Schema)
- Data cleaning and transformation in Power Query
- DAX measures (Gross Profit, Profit Margin %, Average Lead Time)
- Interactive dashboard design and data storytelling

## Key Insights
- **Skincare** is the top-performing product type in both revenue and gross profit.
- Average lead time across the supply chain is **17.08 days**, slightly above the target of 15 days.
- **Road** transportation accounts for the highest share of shipping costs.
- The top 10 SKUs contribute a significant portion of total gross profit, with SKU2 leading.
- Profit margin remains strong at approximately **90.84%**, indicating healthy overall profitability.

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Profit Margin, and Average Lead Time
- Revenue & Gross Profit comparison by Product Type
- Shipping costs breakdown by Transportation Mode
- Top 10 SKUs by Gross Profit
- Average Price and Lead Time analysis by Supplier
- Interactive slicers for Product, Supplier, Location, and Results

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX
- Power Query
